Check your exact Fire TV and Echo combination

The supplied Amazon setup guide describes up to five Echo speakers and one optional Echo Sub, but some Fire TV devices support only two Echo speakers of the same model plus a Sub. Mixed Echo speaker models are not supported. Verify your specific combination before buying or setting up; the diagrams show possible room arrangements, not guaranteed compatibility or discrete audio channels.
