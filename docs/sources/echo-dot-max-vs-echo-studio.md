## Practical takeaway

The graphic presents Echo Dot Max as the smaller, lighter option with room-filling sound, while Echo Studio is larger and heavier and is presented as the more immersive option, with spatial audio and Dolby Atmos. Choose based on room, placement, budget, and intended use, and check that the selected model is compatible with the Fire TV and Alexa Home Theater configuration you plan to use.
