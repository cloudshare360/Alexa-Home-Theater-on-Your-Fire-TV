At these prices, Echo Studio costs approximately **$100 more per speaker**, or **$500 more for five speakers**. This is a separate arithmetic comparison based on the per-speaker prices supplied in the conversation. The cart screenshots instead show four Echo speakers in each configuration.

Amazon's supplied setup information allows up to five Echo speakers in supported Alexa Home Theater configurations, but speaker models cannot be mixed in a configuration. Verify current Fire TV compatibility, exact model generation, and prices before purchasing.

### What the price difference buys

The price comparison alone does not reflect the different speaker hardware. The supplied Echo comparison image lists each Echo Studio with one 3.75-inch woofer and three full-range drivers, as well as spatial audio and Dolby Atmos. It describes the Studio as having “3× bass” compared with Echo Dot Max. Echo Dot Max is listed with one 0.8-inch tweeter and one 2.5-inch woofer. The image does not provide the measurement method for the bass comparison.

The Studio's listed woofer is built into the speaker; it is not the optional separate Echo Sub. Compare the devices based on their full feature sets and intended room experience, not price alone. See [Echo Dot Max vs. Echo Studio](./echo-dot-max-vs-echo-studio.md) for the image-transcribed specifications.
