This guide summarizes the setup information supplied from Amazon's help page. It is a paraphrase for easier reference, not a replacement for Amazon's current compatibility guidance.

## What Alexa Home Theater does

Alexa Home Theater pairs compatible Echo speakers, and optionally an Echo Sub, with a Fire TV device to create a surround-sound experience.

## Supported configurations

The supplied page lists these Fire TV models:

- Fire TV Cube (3rd Generation)
- Fire TV Stick 4K Max (2nd Generation)
- Fire TV Stick 4K (2nd Generation)
- Fire TV Stick 4K Plus

It lists these compatible Echo devices:

- Echo Studio (2025 release)
- Echo Dot Max
- Echo Sub (optional subwoofer)

The page describes configurations with up to five Echo speakers and one optional subwoofer. Mixed Echo speaker configurations are not supported. It also notes that some Fire TV devices support only two Echo speakers of the same model plus a subwoofer. Check the specific Fire TV/Echo combination before purchasing or setting up:

- [Alexa Home Theater Compatibility with Fire TV Devices](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=TWcIor5YmepcPzYSah)

## Before setup

1. Complete setup of the Fire TV device.
2. Complete setup of the Echo devices.
3. Ensure that the devices are connected to the same Wi-Fi network.
4. For an HDMI ARC setup, connect the Fire TV to the TV's HDMI ARC port.
5. Saving the Wi-Fi password to the Amazon account is optional; Amazon says this can help automatically register new devices.

## Setup steps

If you purchased an Alexa Home Theater bundle, follow the Fire TV on-screen setup prompts after finishing Fire TV setup. Otherwise:

1. On Fire TV, open **Settings**.
2. Choose **Display & Sounds**.
3. Choose **Alexa Home Theater**.
4. Choose **Create Alexa Home Theater**.
5. Select the compatible Echo speakers; add an Echo Sub if desired.
6. Follow the placement and calibration prompts:
   - Echo Studio (2025 release) and Echo Dot Max setups require at least two speakers and offer calibration.
   - For other supported Echo configurations, continue to confirm speaker placement as prompted.
7. When setup finishes, choose **Home** or **Watch Preview** to leave setup.

## After setup: audio output and synchronization

Go to **Settings > Display & Sounds > Alexa Home Theater** to adjust the system:

- **Audio Output > Home Theater:** Play and control audio through the Echo speakers.
- **Audio Output > Television:** Control Alexa Home Theater through the television. Amazon recommends this mode for gaming or other latency-sensitive use.
- **Lip Sync Tuning:** Adjust to reduce perceived audio delay.

With a compatible TV and HDMI ARC connection, Alexa Home Theater can also play TV audio and audio from other HDMI-connected sources, such as a game console, through the Echo speakers. Follow the HDMI ARC setup instructions linked above.

## Troubleshooting

### A speaker is missing or unavailable

- Update the software on Fire TV and Echo devices. Ask Alexa to check for Echo software updates; use Amazon's [Fire TV software update instructions](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GTW7A7AUYMKWNVYA) for Fire TV.
- Confirm that Echo speakers are in the same room as the TV and on the same Wi-Fi network.
- If an Echo speaker is absent from the selection screen, choose **Rescan**.
- If a speaker is grayed out, select Echo speakers of the same model; mixed configurations are not supported.

### Echo Sub is missing or updating

- If the Echo Sub is already paired with another Echo speaker, unpair that connection before adding it to the home theater.
- Restart the Sub: unplug it, wait 30 seconds, and plug it back in. The small LED by the power cable should blink orange.
- Use the Alexa app and Amazon's [Set Up Your Echo Sub instructions](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GEFZ4LXXFASPF4MF) to register it.
- After registration, the Sub may update automatically. The supplied help text says this can take 30 minutes or as long as 24 hours. Let the update finish before trying setup again.

### Calibration does not work

- Make sure all Echo speakers are powered and their microphones are unmuted.
- Place speakers around the listening area, near seated ear level, with a clear path for sound. For a two-speaker setup, place one on each side of the TV at the front of the room.
- Put the Echo Sub on the floor near the TV.
- Avoid enclosed or obstructed placements and nearby noise sources such as fans, heaters, and air purifiers.
- For larger rooms, consider moving rear speakers closer to the listening area.
- Keep the room quiet during calibration.
- The supplied instructions also recommend setting the TV audio output to its TV speakers, raising the volume, and checking that sound comes from them.
- If calibration still fails, restart the Echo speakers and Fire TV, then create the home theater again.

It also says mixed Echo speaker models are not supported. Verify the exact Fire TV/Echo pairing against Amazon's current [compatibility list](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=TWcIor5YmepcPzYSah) before purchase or setup.

### Audio quality is poor

- Test playback from more than one streaming service to check whether the issue is specific to a provider.
- If necessary, recreate and recalibrate the system: open **Settings > Display & Sounds > Alexa Home Theater > Edit Alexa Home Theater**, choose **Unpair**, and confirm. Then choose **Create Alexa Home Theater** and follow setup again.
